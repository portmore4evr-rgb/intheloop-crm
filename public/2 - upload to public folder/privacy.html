Privacy Policy

Tuesday Club runs rewards programs on behalf of the restaurant where you signed up ("the Restaurant"). This policy explains what we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • What you give us: first name, mobile number, email (optional), and answers like "What brings you in today?"
  • Visit information: the date and time of each tap or scan, and the rewards and prizes you earn.
  • Basic technical information needed to show the page and keep it secure (browser type, IP address). We do not track your location.
  • A small code saved on your phone so you don't have to re-enter your info next time.

How it's used

  • To count your visits and give you rewards, prizes and streak badges.
  • To send thank-you messages, review requests, streak reminders and offers from the Restaurant by email, and by text only if you checked the text box.
  • To give the Restaurant reports about its program.

Who it's shared with

Only with the Restaurant you joined and with service providers that help us run the program (email delivery, text delivery and hosting), who may use it only for that purpose. We do not sell your information.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Stop texts anytime by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us below. We respond within 30 days.

How long we keep it

While you're a member and for up to 12 months after your last visit, unless the Restaurant ends its program sooner or you ask us to delete it.

Security

Information is stored on secured, password-protected servers. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Children

The program isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Text messages

  • Texts are optional. Joining the rewards program never requires agreeing to texts, and consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • If you check the text box, you'll get up to 4 messages per month from the Restaurant about rewards, reminders and offers.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to cancel at any time, START to rejoin, or HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Program Terms

  • Rewards, prizes and streak prizes are set by the Restaurant and can change or end at any time.
  • One reward code per guest per day. Codes have no cash value and can't be transferred or combined unless the Restaurant says so.
  • A first-time guest's reward starts on their next visit.
  • Rewards and prizes can only be used during the Restaurant's Club Night hours shown on your screen (for example, Mon–Wed, 5pm–8pm). Visits at other times still count, and prizes earned then are saved for 30 days.
  • The weekly free-meal draw has its own Official Rules. No purchase is necessary to enter.
  • The Restaurant may require a minimum order to redeem a reward or prize. When it does, the minimum is shown on your screen with the reward.
  • Weekly streaks count one visit per calendar week (Monday to Sunday).
  • The Restaurant may refuse a code that looks misused, such as a screenshot or a shared phone.
  • If the Restaurant ends its program, unused rewards end with it.
