FAQ

Good questions.

Why can't guests use rewards on a Friday?+

Because you're already full on Friday. Rewards are for filling empty tables, so they only work during the slow nights and hours you choose. Guests still check in on busy nights: the visit counts, and the reward waits for them.

Won't people just share codes?+

Let them. Every reward needs a minimum order you set, like $15 or $20, so a shared code still means a paying guest on a slow night. One reward per person per day.

How does the free-meal draw work?+

Every day a guest checks in, they get one entry. Every Monday morning one winner is picked at random and gets their meal free, up to a cap you choose, on a slow night within 30 days. Official rules are included and no purchase is needed to enter. Have your own lawyer look over the rules if you'd like.

Do my guests need to download an app?+

No. Tapping the tag or scanning the code opens a page in their phone's browser. After the first time, their phone remembers them.

What do my servers have to do?+

Look at the guest's screen. Green means give the reward, pink means a bonus prize, purple means a free meal, yellow means saved for a slow night (nothing to give). A live clock shows it's not a screenshot.

Can guests stop the messages?+

Yes. Guests opt in themselves, every email has an unsubscribe link, and texts stop when they reply STOP.

What happens after the 30 days?+

We'll walk through pricing on your call, so you can decide once you've seen it work.
