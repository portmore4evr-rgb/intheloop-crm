Weekly Free-Meal Draw: Official Rules

No purchase necessary. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

Short version: every day you check in at a participating restaurant's Tuesday Club page, you get 1 entry. Every Monday one entrant per restaurant wins a free meal, up to the value that restaurant sets.

1. Who runs it

Each weekly draw is offered by the participating restaurant whose Tuesday Club page you check in on (the "Restaurant"). Tuesday Club (Worcester, MA) runs the software and picks winners for the Restaurant.

2. Who can enter

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older. Employees and owners of the Restaurant and their immediate households are not eligible for that Restaurant's draw. Void where prohibited.

3. How to enter (free)

Join the Restaurant's Tuesday Club by opening its check-in page (by scanning the code or tapping the tag at the Restaurant, or from a link the Restaurant shares) and giving your first name and mobile number. Each calendar day you check in gives you 1 entry for that week's draw, up to 7 entries a week. No purchase or visit is required: you can check in from anywhere, once per day. Buying food does not give extra entries.

4. Draw period and winner selection

Each draw week runs Monday 12:00am to Sunday 11:59pm Eastern Time. Starting Monday at 9:00am ET, one winner per Restaurant is chosen at random by computer from all eligible entries for the prior week. Your chances depend on how many entries you have and the total number of entries received.

5. The prize

One free meal for the winner at the Restaurant, up to the dollar value shown on the Restaurant's Tuesday Club page (for example, up to $40). Any amount over that value, plus tax and tip where not covered, is paid by the winner. The prize has no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged, and is not combinable with other rewards unless the Restaurant allows it. One prize per person per week.

6. How winners are told and claim the prize

Winners are told by email or text (if they agreed to texts) and on their Tuesday Club page. To claim, visit the Restaurant during its Club Night hours shown on the page, within 30 days of the draw, and check in at your table. Your phone will show a FREE MEAL screen for your server. Unclaimed prizes expire after 30 days and are not redrawn.

7. Winner's name

By entering, you agree the Restaurant may show the winner's first name and last initial (for example, "Ana L.") on its Tuesday Club page. No other personal details are shown.

8. General

Entries made with fake information, multiple identities or automated tools are void. The Restaurant and Tuesday Club may cancel or change the draw if fraud, technical problems or anything else harms its fairness. Winners are responsible for any taxes. By entering you agree to these rules and to our Privacy Policy.

9. Questions or winners list

For questions, or for the name of a past winner, contact the Restaurant or Tuesday Club at the address on our Privacy Policy page.
