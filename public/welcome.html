How it works

From first visit to regular, in six steps.

1

Guests join at the table

They scan a QR code or tap an NFC tag. Name, number, one tap to opt in. No app to download.

2

Reward ID, instantly

Every guest gets their own Reward ID on screen and by text, good for their next visit.

3

Staff redeem next time

Staff type the Reward ID and a 4-digit PIN on any phone or tablet. One tap confirms it's valid.

4

Text your guests

Send a deal to everyone, just new guests, or just regulars, and they hear from you in seconds.

5

Slow nights on autopilot

Pick your slowest night once. Your offer goes out every week without you lifting a finger.

6

One dashboard

See every diner captured and every reward redeemed, all in one place.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do my guests need to download an app?+

No. The check-in page opens right in their phone's browser from the QR code or NFC tag.

What do my staff need?+

Just the redeem page bookmarked on any phone or tablet at the register, plus your restaurant's 4-digit staff PIN.

Can a guest claim the reward twice?+

No. One Reward ID per phone number or email. Rewards can't be used on the sign-up day and only once per guest per day, and every attempt is logged.

Can guests stop the texts?+

Yes. Guests opt in themselves, and replying STOP removes them automatically.
