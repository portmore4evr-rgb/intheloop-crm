FAQ

Good questions.

Do my guests need to download an app?+

No. Tapping the tag or scanning the code opens a page in their phone's browser. After the first time, their phone remembers them.

What do my servers have to do?+

Look at the guest's screen. Green means give the reward, orange means a bonus prize, blue means a new member, gray means already used today. A ticking clock shows it's live, not a screenshot.

Who decides the bonus prizes?+

You do. Pick a prize for the 3rd, 5th and 10th visit and for a 4-week streak. Cheap-to-make items work great, like a dessert, a drink or a side.

Can a guest claim a reward twice?+

No. One member per phone number or email, one reward per day, and nothing on the day they join. Every reward is logged with its code.

Can guests stop the messages?+

Yes. Guests opt in themselves, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
